
Some competitions give you results, some give you lessons, but an event like Formula Bharat '26 stays with you. For many of us, it was our first test, not only of the car, but of patience, belief and how far we pushed ourselves when things went against our way, and yet, when it mattered, everything held. 

Compiled from conversations with the team who lived it — here's what Formula Bharat '26 felt like, in their own words.

![FB26 cover image](/Resources/Blogs/FB26/FB26_2-optimized.jpg "class=max-w-14xsm")

#### "If anybody asks me about FB '26, I'll say brake test" 

The team entered the [brake] testing with the belief of clearing. We were in line to clear the brake test.  
_"We had to do 35 attempts… we cleared it with 15 minutes to spare,"_ Vatsan says—and you can hear how close it was to slipping away. Somewhere between all those attempts, confidence turned into doubt.  
_"Each time he'd [Nikhil, our driver] go, and brake as hard as he could... they'd say "failed!", then he'd go again. Again and again."_ Ruth saw the moment of hope fading. _"Every time he looked at Gopika and gave her a nod… and she would just nod back"._ No words. Just: _again._


Doubt turned into silence. And then, into hope again. _"The moment the wheels locked… and we saw all four judges put their arms up—we screamed."_  
_"People were crying, because all the efforts that were put into the car were basically for that one moment."_


That one moment carried every late night, every fix, and every doubt. Rewarding.



#### What You See v/s What We See 

Behind a successful brake test was everything breaking quietly. Frustration. Strong Opinions. And still choosing the car above all else. _"We literally sat in the hotel and cut the wing plates to clear inspection,"_ Satvik says.  
Not the time to redesign, not the time to optimize. But cut, adjust, fit. Hoping it passes.

![FB26 cover image](/Resources/Blogs/FB26/FB26_1.jpeg "class=max-w-6xsm")  

_"Having contradicting opinions which lead to arguments… but later doing well in the competition is what everyone was working towards,_" Skanda recalls.  
That's the part people don't see.


Even humour found its way in. _"One part of the car you would lick?"_  
_"Front roll hoop… nobody has touched it much,"_ Saaswath says.  
And somehow, that's exactly what this felt like. Exhausted. Delirious. Still joking.



#### The First of our Firsts

For a team that had come painfully close before, completing endurance truly and truly felt earned. It was the first time we saw something we built—run and complete what it was meant to do.  
_"Our mindset was to finish the race,"_ says Neel. No hero laps. No risks. Just bringing it home.
Because this wasn't just another run. _"We never completed endurance before in the club's history"._

The first few laps taken by Nikhil, our first driver, were filled with uncertain drizzles and a hazy helmet. And yet he kept it steady and cool through the remaining 11 laps.
_"I hated going to the second half of the track because it was basically not drivable. So I made my own track, I drove how I could,"_ says the captain, our second driver, who somehow managed to swing a cone up the jacking point, which held more memory to it than the 'mess' it made. 

 ![FB26 image](/Resources/Blogs/FB26/FB26_5-optimized.jpg "class=max-w-6xsm")

#### The Car We Built

It was light. Fast. Aggressive. A proper head-turner. The kind of car people checked out not just for its looks, but how it was built, how it moved, how it roared. There was a feeling that BZR–4 could've done more, and maybe that's true.  
But context matters.

_"Second car to clear TI… first time finishing endurance."_
And that doesn't happen alone.
_"It was possible because of what our seniors did three years back… they laid a strong foundation,"_ says our Team Captain, Neel.  
This wasn't just effort.
It was a legacy continuing. It proved something – that we're getting there, closer than ever.
Not everything went smoothly. There were delays. Missed steps. Things we wish we had done differently.
But here's the thing – we didn't stop. Every issue was accompanied by someone willing to stay back, reevaluate, redo, and push again.

![FB26 cover image](/Resources/Blogs/FB26/FB26_6-optimized.jpg "class=max-w-6xsm")

#### The People Behind It

This wasn't just about subsystems. _"For those who had been here before, it felt like déjà vu. And yet, this time was different."_ Powertrain delivered one of its most reliable performances yet. Solid – the confidence, where even if you're unsure, you stand by it. Because somewhere, there's always truth backing you.  
_{Lochan, Nikhil, Srivatsan, Srianirudhan, Sashank - Powertrain}_

Chassis adapted through redesigns and taking on more than they signed up for. _"We were working on the car until the last minute"._ Amidst the responsibility of major changes and hopes of calculations meeting reality, they trusted each other's capability to fix any issue.  
_{Srinith, Amay, Srajan, Vishnu - Chassis}_

Aero worked through last-minute compliance fixes. Despite the deviation from regulations being small, the team went back that night, disassembled the wings, and manually altered the width to meet the requirements.  
 _"The wings were mostly covered with negative emotions as we weren't able to clear the TI. The reflections we now have are what we plan to strengthen", shared Zion._  
_{Satvik, Srivatsa, Swasti, Zion - Aero}_

![FB26 cover image](/Resources/Blogs/FB26/FB26_10.jpeg "class=max-w-6xsm")

Vehicle Dynamics felt pressure in motion. "_Multiple pushing and pulling of the car. Adjusting. Trying again. Around 20 times before noon. That was the pressure,_" Vismay recalls. Formula Bharat, in one frame, showed a side of celebration, and the other of people breaking down when they couldn't clear. Fixing our hubs, the FS way, sparked us.   
_"Kudos to all our VD boys and our captain Neel - they exhausted all their techniques to clear brakes,"_ recalls Akshay.  
_{Neel, Saaswath, Skanda, Vismaya - Vehicle Dynamics}_

While Electrical had a quite smooth run, the only hitch – the cockpit display did not work.  
_"There was Electrical TI, and there was no reason to worry about it because we were too good,"_ says Lipin.  
_{Akshay, Gopika, Samartha, Abhay, Lipin, Naveen - Electricals}_

At one point, there was barely anyone officially handling management. It didn't matter. People stepped in and did what needed to be done.  
_"The kids showed up… they made sure every monkey in the circus was accounted for,"_ says Ruth.  
And then there was Media. Learning new tools on the spot and experiencing moments through a lens.  
"_I was holding three phones at a time trying to record everything,_" Yuvika says.  
"_We would pass out with our laptops still in our hands,_" Sanjana adds.  
No one stayed in their lane. Because there were no lanes.  
_{Ruth, Tarun, Yuvika, Sanjana - Corporate & Media}_

And sometimes, it was just about calling someone every ten minutes – from the pits to the TI bay to the track – because you needed to know things were okay.  
Even from afar, watching the endurance livestream, one thing stood out– we were loud. Louder than the engines. Louder than the track.  
You could hear us cheering over everything. 
_"We were the only team with the most number of cones,"_ Neel laughs.  
Red. Green. Everywhere.  
And somehow, it became part of the story.  
_"The judges gave us one cone each as a memory."_

#### More Than Just a Competition

FB '26 was nothing like before. For some, it was tougher than expected.  
For others, it was entirely different from Supra.  
For all of us, it imparted a new mindset.  
It gave us something we didn't expect– conversations, connections, and learning from other teams who performed things differently. Emotions that swung from breaking point to breakthrough. We cleared what once stopped us. We tried. Again and again. And when it finally worked, even for a moment, it felt like everything we had been carrying finally moved forward with it.
